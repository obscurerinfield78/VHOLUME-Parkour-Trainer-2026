## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **VHOLUME**. This trainer enhances VHOLUME's movement-focused gameplay with configurable practice and speedrun tools. It helps players explore levels, recover from failed jumps, manage momentum, and improve completion times without adding combat or inventory systems that are not part of the game.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to practice parkour routes, explore the brutalist city, test shortcuts, learn momentum techniques, or improve time-trial performance with optional assistance.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

VHOLUME is built around running, jumping, climbing, sliding, wall movement, and momentum management. Use the trainer's movement options to practice difficult transitions, preserve speed after landings, and test alternate lines without restarting after every mistake. Begin with slower speed settings while learning a route, then gradually remove assistance as your timing improves.

### Tools & Inventory

VHOLUME does not use a conventional inventory or equipment-management system. Progress is centered on completing movement-focused levels, discovering routes, earning performance medals, and improving completion times. The trainer therefore focuses on traversal and practice tools rather than item resources.

### World & Environment

The game takes place in the derelict capital of Afro-Eurasia, a dystopian brutalist city controlled by the Ministry. Levels encourage exploration, momentum-based routing, shortcuts, and experimentation. Use free-exploration and checkpoint options to inspect vertical spaces and learn the intended paths before attempting faster runs.

### Combat & Weapons

VHOLUME has no conventional combat system. Its primary challenge is navigating obstacles and maintaining momentum through precise movement. Trainer options in this category are intended for survival during hazardous drops and difficult parkour sections, not for weapon or enemy manipulation.
